Curated local highlights for Tokyo. Use as reference knowledge when planning
an itinerary -- weave in what's relevant to the traveler's focus and budget
rather than listing everything.

Food:
- Tsukiji Outer Market -- fresh sushi breakfast, street food stalls
- Ichiran Ramen -- solo-booth tonkotsu ramen chain
- Omoide Yokocho (Shinjuku) -- tiny yakitori alleys
- Depachika food halls (basement of any major department store)

Sights & Culture:
- Senso-ji Temple, Asakusa
- Meiji Shrine and surrounding forest, near Harajuku
- Shibuya Crossing and Shibuya Sky observation deck
- teamLab Planets or teamLab Borderless (digital art museum)
- Akihabara for electronics/anime culture
- Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden

Neighborhoods worth wandering:
- Harajuku (Takeshita Street) and Omotesando
- Shimokitazawa for vintage shops and indie cafes
- Ginza for upscale shopping

Practical notes:
- Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card for trains/buses/convenience stores.
- Consider a day trip to Nikko or Kamakura if the trip is 5+ days.
- Many smaller restaurants are cash-only.

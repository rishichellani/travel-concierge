Curated local highlights for New York City. Use as reference knowledge when
planning an itinerary -- weave in what's relevant to the traveler's focus and
budget rather than listing everything.

Food:
- Joe's Pizza (Greenwich Village) -- classic NY slice
- The Halal Guys cart (53rd & 6th) -- chicken over rice
- Katz's Delicatessen (Lower East Side) -- pastrami on rye
- Levain Bakery (Upper West Side) -- famous thick cookies
- Xi'an Famous Foods -- hand-pulled noodles, multiple locations

Sights & Culture:
- Central Park -- especially a morning walk, Bethesda Terrace, The Ramble
- The Metropolitan Museum of Art
- Top of the Rock or Empire State Building for skyline views
- The High Line (elevated park, Meatpacking District to Hudson Yards)
- Brooklyn Bridge walk at sunrise or sunset
- 9/11 Memorial & Museum
- MoMA

Neighborhoods worth wandering:
- Greenwich Village / West Village
- Williamsburg, Brooklyn
- Chinatown / Little Italy

Practical notes:
- Subway is the fastest way around; get a contactless card or OMNY.
- Broadway shows sell same-day rush/lottery tickets for big discounts.

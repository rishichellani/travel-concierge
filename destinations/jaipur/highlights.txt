Curated local highlights for Jaipur. Use as reference knowledge when planning
an itinerary -- weave in what's relevant to the traveler's focus and budget
rather than listing everything.

Food:
- Laxmi Misthan Bhandar (LMB) -- Rajasthani thali, sweets
- Rawat Mishthan Bhandar -- famous pyaaz kachori
- Masala Chowk -- open-air food court with regional stalls

Sights & Culture:
- Amber Fort -- go early morning to beat heat and crowds
- Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds) -- best viewed/photographed from across the street at sunrise
- City Palace and Jantar Mantar (observatory), both in the old city
- Nahargarh Fort -- sunset views over the Pink City
- Johari Bazaar and Bapu Bazaar for jewelry, textiles, handicrafts

Neighborhoods worth wandering:
- The old walled "Pink City" -- markets, gates, architecture

Practical notes:
- March-June is very hot; plan outdoor sightseeing for early morning or evening.
- Bargaining is expected in the bazaars.
- Modest dress recommended at temples and forts.

Curated local highlights for Irvine, CA. Use as reference knowledge when
planning an itinerary -- weave in what's relevant to the traveler's focus and
budget rather than listing everything.

Food:
- Diamond Jamboree / District at Tustin Legacy -- dense cluster of Asian food halls and restaurants
- Irvine Spectrum Center food scene -- wide mix of casual and sit-down chains

Sights & Activities:
- Orange County Great Park -- balloon ride, sports fields, farmers market (weekends), Wild Rivers waterpark in summer
- Irvine Spectrum Center -- shopping, giant Ferris wheel, evening entertainment
- William R. Mason Regional Park -- lake, trails, good for a relaxed morning
- Quail Hill Loop Trail -- easy hiking with hilltop views
- UC Irvine campus and Aldrich Park

Day trips (if trip is 3+ days):
- Newport Beach / Balboa Island -- ~20-30 min away
- Laguna Beach -- ~30 min away, art galleries and coastal views

Practical notes:
- Car is effectively required; it's a car-centric suburban city.
- Weather is mild year-round, so outdoor plans rarely need a backup.

Curated local highlights for Mexico City. Use as reference knowledge when
planning an itinerary -- weave in what's relevant to the traveler's focus and
budget rather than listing everything.

Food:
- El Turix (Roma Norte) -- cochinita pibil tacos, always a line
- Mercado Roma or Mercado San Juan for a food-hall style lunch
- Contramar -- famous tuna tostadas and grilled fish (reservations needed)
- Pujol -- tasting menu, high-end Mexican fine dining
- Street elote and esquites from any corner cart

Sights & Culture:
- Frida Kahlo Museum (Casa Azul), Coyoacan
- Templo Mayor and the Zocalo (historic center)
- Chapultepec Castle and Chapultepec Park
- Museo Nacional de Antropologia
- Xochimilco canal trajinera boat ride
- Teotihuacan pyramids (half-day trip outside the city)

Neighborhoods worth wandering:
- Roma Norte and Condesa -- cafes, parks, art deco architecture
- Coyoacan -- colonial streets, markets, Frida Kahlo's neighborhood

Practical notes:
- Altitude is ~2,240m; pace the first day, hydrate.
- Uber is generally safer/easier than hailing street taxis.
